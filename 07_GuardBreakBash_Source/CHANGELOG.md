# Changelog

## v1.0.0

- Added Bash: successful blocks may stagger the attacker.
- Added GuardBreak: successful blocks may stagger the blocker.
- Added independent stat-reference modes.
- Added configurable probability presets and custom two-stage curves.
- Added configurable limits, midpoint values, cap differences, cooldown, and force.
- Added safe native-template reuse and global fallback.
- Confirmed simultaneous Bash and GuardBreak activation.
- Confirmed operation after save/load and in group combat.
