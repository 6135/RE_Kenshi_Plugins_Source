# Known Limitations

- Added skills are based on the equipment's native `xpTraining` calls. Enabling optional weapon skills does not verify which weapon category the character is actually using.
- Dexterity for lockpicking, assassination and thievery devices is available through SourceData but disabled by default.
- Toughness and most optional combat/weapon skills are disabled by default.
- Only confirmed SourceData entries receive added XP. User-MOD training devices require a SourceData entry.
- This RC does not add or alter FCS buildings, animations or AI behavior.
